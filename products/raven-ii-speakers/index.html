Raven II Speakers by Fern & Roby

The Raven II speakers deliver stunning sound while fitting into the space you have designed to comfortably live in. They fit horizontally on most bookshelves or rest on our custom Speaker Stands.

Designed to deliver something well suited to modern life, the Raven IIs raise the bar in sound quality. The Raven IIs can be driven by low powered tube amplifiers as well as high output solid state. They perform superbly with our integrated solid state Amp No. 2 and all of the LTA amps (my favorite being the Z40i or the Ultralinear Integrated).

The Raven IIs are a single-driver, sealed-box speaker with solid wood cabinets available in ash or walnut.
