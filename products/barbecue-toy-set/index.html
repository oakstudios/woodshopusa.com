Barbecue Toy Set by Melissa and Doug

Fire up the imagination while firing up this pretend play BBQ with all the “fixins” for a fantastic feast! The 20-piece wooden food set includes grilling favorites like hotdog, hamburger, chicken, steak, ribs, veggie burger, and corn, plus tasty toppings and sturdy utensils. Flip the sturdy corrugated box over and it becomes the “grill,” with pictures of knobs for adjusting the temperature and four self-stick tabs to hold the food. The Wooden Grill & Serve BBQ Set promotes hand-eye coordination, creative expression, and imaginative play.
